On usage

Organic usage means your own channels. Paid usage means you can put spend behind it, including allowlisting from my handle. Whitelisting and exclusivity are quoted separately because they cost me future work — I'd rather price that honestly than bury it in a rate card.

On product

Send it to Jacksonville and it gets used on a real car, not staged on a table. If it's a consumable, I'll film enough for the pack in one session so nothing goes to waste. Product isn't payment — it's the thing I'm filming.

Before you ask

The four questions I always get.

Do you post it on your own page?

Only if we agree to it, and it's priced separately. The default is that you get the footage and run it yourself.

How fast?

Five to seven days from the day the product arrives. Rush is possible; ask before you book.

What if the video flops?

You get multiple hooks on the same body specifically so you can swap the opener and re-test without paying for a reshoot. That's where most of the performance lives anyway.

Can you write the script?

Yes, and I'd prefer to. Send talking points and the claims your legal team will allow, and the wording is on me.

Still cheaper than a reshoot.
