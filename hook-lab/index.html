The framework (what each hook needs)
  1. Hook: target a specific audience, evoke an emotion, have interesting visuals.
  2. Problem: the issue the viewer has that relates to the product.
  3. Solution: what the product does that solves it.
  4. CTA: end on the current sale or the restock.
